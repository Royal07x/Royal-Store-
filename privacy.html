Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This basic-version storefront stores your cart, wishlist, account and order data locally in your browser (localStorage) rather than on a remote server. Clearing your browser data will remove this information.

If Royal Store moves to a live backend, this policy will be updated to describe what account, order and delivery data is collected, how long it is kept, and who it is shared with for fulfilment (such as delivery partners).

We do not sell personal data. Location access is only requested when you choose to use "current location" at checkout, and is never collected automatically.

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@royalstore.test.
