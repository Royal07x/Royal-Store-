About Royal Store

Fewer, better things — chosen for fit, fabric and finish.

Royal Store started with a simple frustration: too many wardrobes filled with things that looked good online and disappointed in person. We work directly with small manufacturing partners, test every fit ourselves, and only list what we'd wear.

Today that means four categories — shirts, cargo, sneakers and beauty — each held to the same standard: does this earn a permanent place in someone's routine?

We're a small, remote-first team shipping from India to doorsteps everywhere. If you have questions about a fit, a fabric, or an order, our contact page reaches a real person, not a queue.
